# Implementation notes

Implemented locally on 2026-11-14. React 19.1.1, TypeScript 5.7.3, Vite 6.4.6, MapLibre GL 6.18.0; the explicit ESM worker URL is bundled by Vite. Fonts are bundled Fontsource DM Sans and Pixelify Sans. No paid map key or server is needed.

All 43 original recommendation IDs remain, with the owner's subsequent updates and additions bringing the total to52 entries and32 researched venue branches. Nine park/walk/area guides have explicitly approximate overview anchors, not invented entrances; three walks include named stops and external walking directions, not traced turn-by-turn geometry. Bakeri has two independently saved branches grouped under one parent, with Greenpoint as the default. LIC remains visible with no invented recommendation. Descriptions, personal notes, tips and current visitor-access caveats stay separate; source URLs and dates are retained per location.

The custom palette uses OpenFreeMap/OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap geography. Normal vector-tile buildings appear from zoom14. A local bounded overview layer uses real z14 building footprints sampled/simplified for overview only, with 1.35x display-height exaggeration, not survey-grade geometry. The extraction downloaded50 tiles/17,381,878 bytes at at most4 parallel requests with an app-specific User-Agent, within the provider's inspected download terms. `scripts/prepare-overview.mjs` then `scripts/optimize-overview.mjs` regenerate it. Runtime GeoJSON is5,193,636 bytes. Attribution remains on the map. Ferry is decorative, not live tracking. Reduced motion disables its animation; hidden tabs skip position updates.

Saved entries share canonical IDs across map/list/detail, validate localStorage, recover corrupt shapes and sync cross-tab through storage events. Last successful write wins. Denied storage keeps in-memory saves with a warning. Map failure leaves the list usable; clipboard denial presents copyable text. Directions use the researched address, not the visitor's location.

Known limitations: most brands still have one researched branch, not an exhaustive branch directory; broad routes do not provide turn-by-turn geometry. Thirty-two entries have openly licensed real local photos with source credits;20 use original category illustrations. Before the requested Vercel deployment, the owner chose licensed photos only:18 unconfirmed official-site photographs were removed from public assets. Overview buildings remain sampled and bounded, and public map tiles have no SLA. Production JS is split: the entry chunk (UI, list, data; about 289KB, 82KB gzip) renders the controls and list first, and the MapLibre chunk (about 1.26MB, 353KB gzip) loads straight after it, plus the 519.6KB map worker. MapLibre's CSS stays in the entry stylesheet, imported before the app CSS, so the app's map rules keep overriding it. List thumbnails are 180px copies in `public/images/places/thumbs/` (regenerate with `node scripts/make-thumbnails.mjs`); detail views use the full photo. `node --experimental-websocket scripts/measure-load.mjs` measures first load of a running preview at desktop and throttled mobile (1.6 Mbps, 150 ms, 4x CPU) settings. Ordinary single-location places marked `"editableIn": "docs/content/places.csv"` are maintained through that CSV and `npm run places`; Brunch and Dinner are search tags under Food, not separate filters. Existing history tracks node_modules; generated dependency noise was not cleaned or included in these updates. The original unused malformed Header is outside the application/typecheck graph.

## Illustrated Greenpoint pilot (map redesign)

- **Camera.** The map is locked to pitch 52° and bearing 38° (`MAP_PITCH`/`MAP_BEARING` in `src/map-style.ts`). At that angle Greenpoint's near north–south grid runs diagonally on screen, matching the 2:1 isometric sprites. Drag-rotate, pitch-with-rotate, touch pitch, touch rotation and keyboard rotation are disabled; pan and zoom remain. Every camera action (overview, place, neighborhood, walk stop, clusters, walks, Saved, Explore Greenpoint, Find Eda reveal) passes the same angle, and `fitBounds` no longer flattens the map first.
- **Basemap.** Softer cream/butter paper, blue water with a shoreline glow, sage/pistachio parks. Minor streets fade in from z13.4; mid streets from z11.5; major streets and bridges stay visible. Overview buildings are flat pastel footprints with only ≥90 m towers extruded; from z14 buildings are flat fills with only ≥40 m buildings extruded. Attribution is unchanged.
- **Art assets.** `src/map-art/sprites.ts` holds original SVG artwork drawn by a small isometric helper: six brownstone/storefront variants, three café/bakery storefronts with striped awnings and outdoor tables, a rooftop garden, two trees, planter, bench, café tables, dog walker, coffee walker, cyclist, picnic, pigeon with a bagel, ferry, steam and the Find Eda placeholder avatar. The browser rasterizes these fixed SVG strings into MapLibre images once on load. Nothing is generated per visitor and no AI or paid service is involved. The sprites are decorative and do not depict any real business.
- **Placement.** `scripts/build-greenpoint-scene.mjs` is a one-off developer script. It downloads 4 OpenFreeMap z14 tiles, places sprites on real OSM building footprints and sidewalks with a fixed seed, and writes `src/map-art/greenpoint-scene.json` (589 items). Density fades out from about 340 m to 860 m around central Greenpoint, so the pilot has no hard edge. Sprites stay at least 42 m from every venue coordinate, and café sprites stay at least 120 m away, so they never suggest a venue's location. Eda and the picnic sit on the Transmitter Park lawn.
- **Rendering.** Static scenery is one MapLibre symbol layer (`gp-scene`), drawn under road names and under all HTML pins and labels. It is not interactive, so it cannot steal clicks. Only five DOM decorations exist: the ferry, three steam wisps and one dog with a wagging tail. All of them have `pointer-events:none`. The ferry position updates at about 12 fps. CSS pauses ambient animation while the tab is hidden, and `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all of it.
- **Find Eda.** This is an optional game (`src/components/FindEda.tsx`). It has a clue, a "Reveal her spot" action that moves the camera to her and focuses her button for keyboard users, and a short celebration. Completion is stored in `edas-new-york:find-eda:v1`, separate from Saved.
- **Regression.** `scripts/check-illustrated-map.mjs` checks the fixed angle after each camera action, confirms the Greenpoint pins are clickable and not covered, checks that Find Eda persists separately from Saved, and checks reduced motion and mobile.
- **Limits.** Detailed art covers only the Greenpoint pilot. Tall real towers (for example on the Greenpoint waterfront) still render as soft extrusions. Waldo reference ideas (seeded variation, a small CPU animation loop, reduced motion) were adapted conceptually. No upstream code was copied.


## Greenpoint desktop miniature (2026-11-15)

This supersedes the earlier illustrated-pilot camera and architecture notes above. Desktop opens at zoom 17.35, pitch 55°, bearing 38°, around [-73.9565, 40.7318]. A 26° vertical field of view reduces perspective distortion. All New York remains available as a secondary view; Back to Greenpoint and clearing Explore filters return to the neighborhood.

Building and tree billboards are replaced with real MapLibre fill-extrusion geometry. The checked-in geographic extract supplies walls, roof caps, coping, windows, stoops, striped awnings, café furniture, planters and layered tree crowns. The geometry uses the same projection and depth buffer as the map, so there is no second renderer or camera to synchronize. Only small character accents remain sprites; unused architecture sprites are no longer rasterized on load. Steam anchors come from generated café tables. All named places retain their existing researched coordinates.

The artwork is compiled by `node scripts/build-greenpoint-miniature.mjs`, never generated per visitor. That script makes four bounded tile downloads plus a TileJSON lookup and writes the public GeoJSON and source metadata. The map still fetches the existing free OpenFreeMap basemap and fonts in the browser. No paid map, AI service, new package or backend was added. The asset is approximately 6.3 MB raw / 0.52 MB gzip; actual transfer depends on the host's compression. Detailed façades are limited to a 165 m radius; simple surrounding buildings provide context. Tiny geometry uses zero GeoJSON simplification tolerance so window rectangles survive tiling.

These are stylized buildings, not measured façades. Building footprints and street centerlines derive from OpenStreetMap, but heights are capped, colors and storefront details are invented, and scenery does not identify real businesses or amenities. The derived data is ODbL; attribution remains on the map and in the metadata. The wider city retains the previous map treatment. No map-service terms or host configuration were changed.

Desktop controls, panels, typography, labels and the introduction are smaller. The introduction dismisses on pan, user zoom, search/filter interaction, place selection or explicit close. Save storage and Find Eda storage schemas are unchanged. Ambient motion remains limited to the ferry, steam and dog, with existing reduced-motion and hidden-tab handling.

Validation and remaining browser limitation: see [Greenpoint desktop review](GREENPOINT_DESKTOP_REVIEW.md). Do not describe the 3D view as browser-verified until that checklist is completed on a WebGL2-capable browser.
