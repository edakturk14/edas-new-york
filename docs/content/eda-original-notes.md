## A few notes before you start

- **Subway:** You don’t need a MetroCard. Just tap a contactless credit/debit card or your phone at the entrance.
- **Google Maps is generally good for subway directions**, but note that sometimes there are alerts or changes in schedules, especially on the weekends.
- **Reservations:** For popular restaurants, they need a Rez!

## Outdoors / walks

- **Central Park**
- **Brooklyn Bridge Park + Brooklyn Heights**
- **Brooklyn Bridge** — Walk **Brooklyn → Manhattan**
- **The High Line** — Elevated park through Chelsea. Touristy but still worth doing once. Combine it with Chelsea Market/Meatpacking/West Village.
- **West Side Highway / Hudson River Park** — Especially nice around sunset.
- **Prospect Park**
- **Greenpoint waterfront** — Transmitter Park → Greenpoint Ferry

## Neighborhoods worth wandering

- **West Village** — cafes, rest, pictures
- **SoHo + Nolita** — Shopping, architecture, cafés. Good in the afternoon.
- **Lower East Side** — energetic and young
- **East Village** — restaurants
- **Williamsburg**
- **Brooklyn Heights + DUMBO** — Very pretty, very NYC, perfect for first-time visitors.
- **Upper West Side** — Central Park + Natural History. Much more residential.
- **Upper East Side / Museum Mile** — Met/Guggenheim + Central Park + classic old Manhattan.

## Commute Greenpoint

- Easy access to Manhattan with the NYC ferry (can’t use credit card, must get the NYC ferry app and buy tickets on there).
- Brooklyn: The G subway.
- Airport: Subway (but requires 2 different subways, so if you have big luggage or if the time is late, not recommended).
